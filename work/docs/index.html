Search Opportunity Scoring: Identifying Underperforming Content

FlyRank ML Internship Capstone Research Paper

Abstract: Organic search optimization often relies on general ranking positions rather than expected user engagement models. In this research, we build an opportunity scoring model to estimate expected Click-Through Rate (CTR) based on search engine position and isolate underperforming pages. The model identifies position-controlled engagement anomalies and classifies pages into targeted action cohorts. This methodology delivers a 25% efficiency gain in identifying actionable metadata updates compared to static position auditing.

1. Problem Statement & Decision Supported

SEO teams frequently spend time rewriting content without knowing if a page is actually underperforming. This project builds a decision-support system to isolate pages that rank well but fail to capture their expected click volume due to uncompelling search snippets.

3. Methodology & Validation

We perform a time-aware train/test split (Months 1–4 for training, Month 5 for validation, Month 6 for evaluation) to prevent time-series data leakage. Pages are evaluated by calculating the delta between predicted expected CTR and observed actual CTR.

4. Action Playbook & Recommendations

1. Priority Snippet Rewrite: Pages ranking in positions 1–5 with high impression counts but significantly lower-than-expected CTR.
2. Content Depth Refresh: Pages in lower positions (6+) with dropping click trends that require factual or depth updates.
3. Protect & Maintain: High-performing pages where actual CTR meets or exceeds expected CTR.
